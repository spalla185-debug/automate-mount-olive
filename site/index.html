Privacy Policy

Last updated October 13, 2026

Automate Mount Olive ("we," "us") is an independent business based in Mount Olive, New Jersey. This policy explains what we collect through this website and our services, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email, business type, and anything you write in the message box.
  • Booking: when you schedule through Calendly, Calendly collects your name, email, and chosen time under its own privacy policy, and shares the booking with us.
  • Basic site data: our host (Vercel) may log standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, for security and performance.
  • Client data: if you become a client, we access only the tools and data you authorize so we can build and run your automations.

How we use it

To reply to you, schedule your consultation, deliver and maintain the services you sign up for, and send you service-related messages. Form submissions are processed by an automated workflow (n8n) that stores your details in a private Google Sheet and sends you a confirmation email. We do not sell, rent, or reuse your information for advertising.

Services we rely on

Vercel (website hosting), n8n Cloud (workflow automation), Google Workspace (email and storage), and Calendly (scheduling). Each processes data under its own privacy terms. For clients, we also connect to tools you already use, such as your CRM, calendar, or inbox, only with access you grant.

Security and retention

We limit access to the people who need it and use providers that encrypt data in transit and at rest. We keep lead information for up to 24 months after our last contact, and client data for as long as you remain a client, then delete it on request or at the end of service. You can revoke our access to your tools at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct, or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link. This site is intended for businesses and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. Questions or requests: automatemountolive@gmail.com
