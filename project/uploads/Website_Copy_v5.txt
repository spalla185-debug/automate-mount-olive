FAQ
========================================
FAQ
Fair questions, straight answers.

Q: You're high school students. Why should we trust you with our business?
A: Fair question. You can see exactly what we built and for whom in the Proof section. You start with a free 30-minute consult and a 14-day trial, so you're not betting anything big up front, and you can cancel in one click. We only connect to the tools you already use, with access you control.

Q: We already have something in place.
A: Good. We're not asking you to rip it out. In the free consultation we figure out what's actually working and automate around it, or replace only the piece that's costing you time. If your current setup already does the job, we'll tell you that too.

Q: This seems expensive.
A: Compare it to the hours you spend every week on follow-ups and scheduling. Setup is a flat one-time fee, and the monthly cost is less than a part-time hire. You also get a 14-day free trial and half off your first billed month, so you'll know it's worth it before you pay full price.

Q: Is our data safe?
A: We keep what we touch to a minimum. Workflows run on n8n Cloud, which encrypts stored data at rest (AES-256) and keeps each customer's instance separate. We only connect to the tools you already use, with access you control, and you can revoke it anytime. We don't sell or reuse your data. If you handle sensitive client info, like legal intake, we map out exactly what the automation touches in the free consult before we build anything.
